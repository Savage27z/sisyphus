HOW TO PLAY

  1. Everyone starts at the bottom. Climb all twelve levels to the top: Mount Olympus, The Tower, the Construction Site or the Haunted Hotel.
  2. Every level is packed with obstacles: pistons, wrecking balls, rockfalls, lasers, ghosts, crumbling floors and spinning bars. There's always more than one way up.
  3. You start with a Blaster. Shots knock people back, and enough damage knocks them down a level or two. Nobody is ever eliminated.
  4. The orange weapon racks on every level hold better guns (Shotgun, Rocket, Railgun, Freeze ray) and melee weapons. They refill. The two by the start always hold a Rocket or a Railgun, so it's a race.
  5. Supply drops parachute in through the round: a weapon plus a trap.
  6. Grab the flag at the top and hold it for 3 seconds to win. Whoever holds the flag is harder to knock away, but a real knockdown makes them drop it.
  7. PLAY ONLINE drops you into the next public round (up to 24 players, bots fill the gaps). Rounds with enough real players are prize rounds.
  8. Play with friends: make a room and send the code or invite link.
